How the policy works

Tier 0: auto-approved

Low-risk viewer roles, no sensitive data, expiry within 30 days.

Tier 1: manager

Standard roles, restricted systems, longer viewer access, or any request whose text tried to instruct the system.

Tier 2: manager + app owner

Admin roles and any role that can read sensitive data.

Always

Every grant expires. No one approves their own request. The agent can't approve. Contractors can't hold admin or sensitive roles. Payment creator and approver can't be combined.
